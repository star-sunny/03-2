CONFERENCE DETAIL

About the Conference

First international conference on materials design manufacturing and thermal analysis (ICADMAT-2026) will be organized by Bhagalpur College of Engineering, Bhagalpur during April 17-18, 2026. As this is the first edition of the conference, it aims to bring together academicians, researchers, and industry professionals to share their insights and advancements in various fields of engineering and technology.

Selected high-quality research papers will be considered for publication in Scopus indexed journals/ Books Chapter/ Conference proceedings. The conference also seeks to establish collaborations with reputed publishers and journals for future editions.

ICADMAT-2026 will feature three parallel technical tracks, covering diverse engineering disciplines. The best papers in each track will be recognized with Best Paper Awards. Additionally, all accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings with an ISBN number, while selected quality papers may be published in Scopus-indexed journals and SCI indexed journal.

About Bhagalpur College of Engineering (BCE), Bhagalpur

Established in 1960, Bhagalpur College of Engineering (BCE) is a premier government engineering institution under the Department of Science, Technology and Technical Education, Government of Bihar. Affiliated with Bihar Engineering University (BEU), Patna, and approved by AICTE, New Delhi , the college offers B.Tech and M.Tech programs across various disciplines. With modern laboratories, experienced faculty and a strong research culture, BCE promotes innovation and excellence in technical education. Over six decades, it has produced competent engineers serving nationally and globally. The institution actively organizes conferences, seminars, and workshops, contributing to technological advancement and academic growth.

About Bhagalpur

Bhagalpur, popularly known as the "Silk City of Bihar", is one of the oldest cities in India, located on the southern bank of the river Ganga. The city is famous for its traditional Tussar silk, rich cultural heritage, and historical significance as part of the ancient Anga Kingdom. Bhagalpur is also home to the Vikramsila University ruins, a renowned center of Buddhist learning, reflecting its glorious academic past.

Surrounded by natural beauty, the city features the Vikramsila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary, housing India's national aquatic animal. Today, Bhagalpur is an emerging educational and industrial hub, blending tradition with modernity. Its excellent connectivity by rail and road to major cities like Patna, Kolkata, and Delhi makes it an ideal destination for national and international academic events.

📢 Call for Papers

ICADMAT 2026 (International Conference on Materials Design, Manufacturing and Thermal Analysis)

ICADMAT 2026 is an attempt to discuss the advances in the field of Mechanical Sciences applied to materials and manufacturing. We invite researchers, scientists, engineers, and students from around the world to submit their original, unpublished research papers.

Submit Your Paper

List of Topics

Topics include, but are not limited to, the following themes across five tracks:

Track 1: Advanced Material

  • Nanostructured & Nanomaterials
  • Composite Materials, Glass and Ceramic Materials
  • Light Metals and Alloys, Steel Alloys, Shape Memory Alloys
  • Heat Treating, High Temperature Materials, Biomaterials
  • Functionally Graded Materials, Forming Processes, Coatings & Coating Techniques
  • Optical and Electronical Materials, Noise & Acoustics

Track 2: Materials Manufacturing and Processing

  • Materials Synthesis, Processing and application; Material Characterization
  • Advanced Metal Forming, Bending, Welding & Casting Technique, Powder Metallurgy
  • High-Speed Machining, Hybrid Machining, Non-conventional Machining Processes
  • Additive Processing and Joining Technologies, Chemical Analysis
  • Surface Engineering for Manufacturing Process
  • Design and Behavior of Equipment and Tools; Surface Treatment and Machining
  • Computational Modeling and Simulation

Track 3: Engineering Optimization and Sustainable Development

  • Application of Artificial Intelligence in Manufacturing Sector, Industry 4.0
  • Advanced Optimization Techniques
  • Sustainable Environmental Materials
  • Non-Destructive Evaluation Techniques
  • Innovation in Sustainable Technology, Performance Optimization
  • Energy & Environmental Materials

Track 4: Tribology for Industrial Application

  • Bearing & Gear Tribology, Lubricants & Additives
  • Contact Mechanics & Friction, Wear Mechanisms, Tribo-Testing
  • Tribology in Design & Manufacturing, Tribology in Metalworking
  • Bio Tribology, Nano Tribology, Space Tribology
  • Failure Analysis, Automotive Tribology, Marine Tribology
  • Wind Power Tribology, Tribology in Renewable Energy, Innovations in Tribology

Track 5: Thermal Analysis of Materials for Industrial Application

  • Advanced Techniques in Thermal Analysis
  • Thermal Properties of Industrial Materials
  • Thermal Analysis for Manufacturing and Processing
  • Thermal Management and Energy Applications
  • Thermal Analysis for Sustainability and Recycling
  • Simulation and Modeling, Case Studies and Industrial Implementation

📝 Full Paper Submission Guidelines

  • The manuscript should be written in .doc/.docx format in English with a maximum paper length of eight pages (including tables, diagrams, figures, illustrations, equations, etc.).
  • After submission of your paper through CMT, you will receive an ID that must be used for all further communications.
  • All submitted papers will undergo initial screening by the Technical Program Committee, which includes checks for the scope relevance, plagiarism, manuscript organization, and quality of tables/figures/illustrations.
  • Authors will be required to submit the camera-ready paper and a signed copyright form prior to their presentation.
  • The schedule for paper presentations and contests will be communicated in advance.

📚 Publication Opportunities
